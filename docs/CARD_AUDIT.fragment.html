How the 100 points are made

Practical utility 20Rated 0 to 5: how much it really helps you get something done, by the effort, time or mistakes it saves. 5 changes what you do every day; 4 most weeks; 3 sometimes; 2 is mostly information; 1 is nice to know; 0 changes nothing. A copy is rated on its function, and judged by the distance rule.
Unique purpose 15Rated 0 to 5. 5 is the only place AND essential; 4 is the only place; 3 overlaps a little; 1 mostly repeats another; 0 repeats it fully.
Distance 20Taps to the other place that does the same job. None 20, 6 or more 16, 4 to 5 12, 3 taps 5, 2 taps 2, 0 to 1 tap 0. A copy is allowed only at 4 or more.
Moment 10Rated 0 to 5. 5 is time-critical and shows exactly then; 3 is useful when it shows; 1 is rarely needed.
How often 10Rated 0 to 5. 5 is many times a day; 3 is a few times a week; 1 is about monthly.
Room it takes 5Rated 0 to 5. 5 is a line or a small button; 1 is a big card. Less room scores higher.
What breaks without it 10Rated 0 to 5. 5 is safety, proof or blocking only; 4 the Buffer or money; 3 a main workflow; 1 a convenience; 0 nothing.
Rule behind it 10A Locked rule names it 10, a Default rule 4, no rule 0.
Verdict 60 / 40Pinned by a Locked rule: keep. The same job within 3 taps of another place: merge, whatever the score. Otherwise 60 or more keeps. Below 60 with no other place: a unique purpose rated 0 or 1, or under 40, deletes; anything else moves down. Within 4 points of a line is marked borderline.

The scores are my judgement from reading the code, and every number is in its row, so any of them can be overruled and the total moves with it. Ratings are harsh: 5 is exceptional and 3 is ordinary. Rules are named by their ledger id: P is a Locked rule that names the card, A is a Default rule that would be amended in the same change. Widgets, notifications and the lock screen repeat Home facts on purpose (B2) and are not scored as duplicates. The breakdown reads: practical utility, unique purpose, distance, moment, how often, room, what breaks, rule.
