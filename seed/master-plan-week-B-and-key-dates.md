### The jump to a First

Your Year 2 average is 47.3 and the degree weights Year 2 to Year 3 as 1 to 3. A 2:2 needs a Year 3 average of about 50.9, a 2:1 about 64.2 and a First about 77.6. That is a jump of roughly 30 points from Year 2, and your best Year 2 mark was 53. It is possible for a student who makes a real leap, and it is rare; this plan gives the effort its best chance, but nothing here can promise it. Hold a 2:1 (64) as the floor you defend, and let the timed past-paper average in the Test Log tell you the truth every fortnight. If it is under 55 at the end of November, change the method (Red Engine and office hours), not just the hours.
