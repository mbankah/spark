How the score works

U, unique purpose, 0 to 33 is the only place that answers its question. 0 is a card nothing needs.
M, moment, 0 to 33 is needed exactly when it shows. 1 is rarely needed.
D, distance, 0 to 33 means nothing else does the job. 2 means the other place is 4 or more taps away, so a copy is allowed. 1 is 2 to 3 taps. 0 is one tap or less.
Verdict7 to 9 keep. 4 to 6 fold into another card or shelf. 0 to 3 delete. A card a Locked rule names is pinned: it is never deleted, only simplified.
