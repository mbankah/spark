Spark CSV prompt

Get an AI to turn your timetable or deadlines into a file Spark can import. Works in any browser, on any phone or computer. Nothing here is sent anywhere: the check runs on this page.

1. Copy this prompt

Paste it into any AI app, then paste your timetable or dates underneath. Ask it to reply with the CSV only.

2. Paste what the AI gave you

3. Save it and give it to Spark

  • Android: open the saved file with Spark (Open with), or share it to Spark, or Setup, Import a timetable file.
  • Windows, Mac, Linux: send the file to your phone (email, a cloud drive, a USB cable, any messaging app) and open it with Spark there. The PC does not import files itself: your phone is the source of truth.

Spark checks every line again against your calendar and shows a preview. Nothing changes until you press Apply. A file that cancels something needs the typed wall.
